# References

Complete bibliographic record for every source cited in the codebase,
docstrings, and documentation of this project.

## Color science

- Mahy, M., Van Eycken, L., & Oosterlinck, A. (1994). Evaluation of
  uniform color spaces developed after the adoption of CIELAB and
  CIELUV. *Color Research & Application, 19*(2), 105–121.
  https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1520-6378.1994.tb00070.x

- Ottosson, B. (2020, December 23). *A perceptual color space for image
  processing* [Blog post; matrices revised 2021-01-25].
  https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/

- Sharma, G., Wu, W., & Dalal, E. N. (2005). The CIEDE2000
  color-difference formula: Implementation notes, supplementary test
  data, and mathematical observations. *Color Research & Application,
  30*(1), 21–30. https://doi.org/10.1002/col.20070

## Notes

- The APCA entry documents the experimental contrast metric this project
  reports for research comparison; it is not a WCAG 2.2 conformance
  criterion and must not be cited as one.
- The ISO Coated v2 (ECI) profile is bundled in this repository;
  `color_analysis_tool/profiles/README.md` carries its provenance,
  license note, and checksum.
